Privacy

Last updated October 2026

The short version

This website does not use cookies, analytics, ad pixels or any other tracking. We do not sell or rent personal information.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us:

  • Contact form. Your name, email, company (if given), area of interest and message are stored in a Google Form that only Rector & Stull can access. We use them only to reply to you.
  • Margin waitlist. Your email address is stored the same way. We use it only to send Margin updates, and you can ask to be removed at any time.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. Form submissions are sent to Google Forms, which stores them on our behalf. No other third-party services are used.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete anything you've sent us, email hello@rectorandstull.com.
